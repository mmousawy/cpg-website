- Members set shooting formats and a daily-driver body/lens, then keep an inventory at /account/gear
- Public profile shows a short summary; the full list is at /@nickname/gear
- Inventory grouped by type gets an "Add …" button that opens the form with that type already selected
- Sort by type, name, recently added, or a custom drag order
- Achievement carousel hides the previous/next buttons when that direction cannot scroll
