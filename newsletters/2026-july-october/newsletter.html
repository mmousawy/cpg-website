A lot has landed on the site since our last newsletter that was sent mid-July! Here's an overview of all the new stuff on the platform.

Find people who shoot the way you do

You can now put your photography kit on your profile.

Under My gear, add what you shoot with: camera bodies, lenses, film, phones, lighting, supports, and accessories. Mark a daily driver, and choose how you shoot - digital, analog, phone, instant, drone, video, toy and lo-fi, or something else.

Shooting styles and daily drivers show on your profile once you pick at least one.

On the Members page, two new sections use that information:

  • "Shoots on" groups people by format.
  • "Daily drivers" shows the camera bodies and lenses members reach for most often.

Every member with a gear profile gets a public page at @nickname/gear. The gear page lists your shooting style, daily driver body and lens, and you full gear inventory grouped by type.

A guided tour of your photo library

The first time you open Photos, a short tour walks you through the library: drag-and-drop upload, titles, tags, license, and public versus private. New uploads still start private. Public photos show in the gallery and on your profile.

Once you have photos, a second tour covers selecting several at once, editing them together, adding them to an album, reordering, and filtering All / Public / Private. Once you close the tour, it will not keep coming back.

Comments you can edit

You can now edit your comments after you have posted them. The reply flow has been streamlined a bit as well by being clearer where your reply will be placed in the comment thread.

Notifications from people you follow

You can now follow people! Following means that you get notifications such as "Alex uploaded 12 new photos". This way you can keep up with your favourite photographers on the platform!

In the weekly digest, you get notified about anything you still have not read from the past week: member uploads, likes, comments, and new followers. It shows a few of them and links to your activity page. Notifications you already opened in the app stay out of the email, and you can turn the digest off in your email settings.

Photos you upload in private do not trigger any notifications.

Sharing, events, and profiles

Photos, events, and challenges have a share button: copy the link, or send it through WhatsApp, Facebook, X, or Pinterest. On a phone, it uses the system share sheet.

Event pages embed a map for the meetup location, and the location line is cleaner.

You can now change your nickname in the platform, and needs to be confirmed by a link in your email.

Nicknames

If you ever change your nickname, old profile links redirect to the new one, so previously shared URLs keep working for a full year. You can change your nickname once every 60 days.

Onboarding

The onboarding flow is completely rebuilt. There is now a clearer intro, and you are guided through a couple of steps to set up your account.

New look on mobile devices

The main navigation menu has been moved to the bottom of the screen with clear tabs. This makes it easier to navigate the platform with one hand

Photo grids flow more evenly on smaller screens. Profile stats and badges were tightened up.

More customizabilty and appearance settings

New theme added: Midnight - A deeper and more mysterious dark theme! 🌑

The new settings at a glance:

  1. Photo grid style Justified rows that follow each photo’s shape, or uniform square tiles. Comfortable for larger previews, or Compact to fit more on screen.
  2. Photo grid density: Show larger or smaller thumbnails in overviews.
  3. Photo captions: Titles on hover, or titles and the photographer’s name always visible.
  4. Motion: Follow your device settings, or keep platform animations reduced.

Photo colors, and a note for organizers

Image processing now keeps ICC color profiles through WebP conversion, that means that photo thumbnail colors should now look identical to the original file you uploaded.

In the back-office, there are more tools for organizers: they now have an admin statistics dashboard: member activity over time, interests, email opt-outs, themes, and storage to keep track of the community.

Check out the platform!

My gear is the place to start. Select how you shoot and fill in the camera you carry. Each camera someone adds makes the platform more fun!
